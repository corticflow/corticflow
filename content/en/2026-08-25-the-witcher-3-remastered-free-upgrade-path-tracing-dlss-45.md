CD Projekt Red took the stage during the Opening Night Live at Gamescom 2026 to deliver an astonishing update for RPG enthusiasts: *The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt* is getting a massive, ground-up remastered edition set for release on September 29. Even more surprising is the studio’s decision to offer this comprehensive overhaul entirely free of charge to anyone who already owns the base game.

More than a decade after its original debut redefined modern open-world game design, Geralt of Rivia’s journey is receiving a technological and mechanical transformation. *The Witcher 3: Remastered* goes beyond standard texture upsizing, integrating cutting-edge graphics technologies, completely refactored traversal and combat mechanics, redesigned monster behaviors, and a reworked control scheme for Geralt’s trusted steed, Roach.

### Next-Gen Rendering: Path Tracing, DLSS 4.5, and Advanced Upscaling

From a technical hardware perspective, *The Witcher 3: Remastered* serves as a showcase for current and upcoming PC graphics architectures. CD Projekt Red has implemented full Path Tracing—the ultra-demanding ray-tracing method that simulates real-time physics of light across all environment surfaces—for high-end PC rigs. This brings real-time bounces, realistic micro-shadows, and accurate dynamic global illumination to the forests of Velen and the vibrant city streets of Novigrad.

To accommodate these rendering workloads across diverse hardware configurations, the remaster integrates the latest hardware-accelerated upscaling suites:
- **NVIDIA DLSS 4.5:** Utilizing multi-frame generation and deep-learning reconstruction to sustain high framerates under heavy Path Tracing workloads.
- **AMD FSR 4.0 & Intel XeSS 2.0:** Offering machine-learning-driven spatial and temporal upscaling options across diverse GPU architectures.

Beyond high-end ray tracing, general visual fidelity has been upgraded across the board. The remaster introduces revamped high-resolution assets, dense foliage rendering, enhanced volumetric weather effects, and native High Dynamic Range (HDR) recalibration.

### Gameplay Mechanics, Combat, and Traversal Overhauls

CD Projekt Red has addressed several legacy mechanics that felt aged by modern standards. The traversal framework has been rebuilt from scratch: Geralt can now fluidly vault over obstacles, scale ladders seamlessly, and interact with environmental geometry without the clunky animations of the 2015 original.

Combat and character progression have also received significant tuning:
- **Refined Magic Signs:** Visual and functional overhauls to dynamic sign casting.
- **Skill Tree & Item Rebalancing:** Streamlined itemization, updated mutagen bonuses, and reworked talent trees to encourage diverse build paths.
- **Monster AI:** Enhanced pathfinding, aggressive tactics, and varied counter-attack routines for beasts and boss encounters.
- **Roach (Carpeado) Controls:** A top-to-bottom re-engineering of equine movement dynamics, eliminating sudden stopping bugs and improving path following on narrow trails.

### Free Expansions and the 2027 'Songs of the Past' Expansion

In a rare gesture for modern AAA publishing, CD Projekt Red confirmed that all current owners of the base game will automatically receive both major expansions—*Hearts of Stone* and *Blood and Wine*—completely free, permanently upgrading their licenses to the equivalent of the original Complete Edition alongside the remastered improvements.

Looking further ahead, CD Projekt Red also unveiled gameplay footage for a brand-new story expansion titled *Songs of the Past*, scheduled for release in 2027. Set in the uncharted region of Letten, the expansion promises to introduce fresh mutagens, formidable enemy archetypes, and a versatile chain weapon designed for medium-range tactical combat.

### Launch Platforms and Availability

*The Witcher 3: Remastered* launches globally on September 29 for PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and the Nintendo Switch 2, with digital pre-orders already live on Nintendo's platform.
