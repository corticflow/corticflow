title: "Brazil's Data Privacy Authority Fines ByteDance R$ 153.7 Million Over TikTok Minor Protection Failures"
date: "2026-08-25"
category: "Business & Startups"
---

<p>In one of the most severe regulatory actions taken against a global technology giant in South America, Brazil&rsquo;s National Data Protection Authority (ANPD) has imposed a landmark fine of <strong>R$ 153.7 million</strong> (approximately $27 million USD) on ByteDance, the parent company behind TikTok. Announced on Tuesday, the historic sanction stems from an extensive administrative investigation that exposed systemic irregularities in how the short-form video platform processed the personal data of children and adolescents.</p><h2>A Detailed Breakdown of the Regulatory Infractions</h2><p>The enforcement action was formally spearheaded by the ANPD&rsquo;s Superintendence of Enforcement following an investigation initiated in November 2024. According to official regulatory findings, ByteDance systematically engaged in the processing of minor data without establishing a valid legal basis as required under Brazil&rsquo;s General Data Protection Law (LGPD).</p><p>Crucially, regulators discovered that these unlawful data collection practices occurred across two distinct user experiences:</p><ul><li><strong>Logged-in user profiles:</strong> Where minor accounts were subjected to automated data collection and behavioral profiling without adequate legal consent or parental verification mechanisms.</li><li><strong>Guest/Logged-out feeds:</strong> Where unregistered visitors—including young children—were tracked and targeted without effective barriers or explicit legal coverage.</li></ul><p>Furthermore, the ANPD identified critical technical and procedural failures in TikTok&rsquo;s age verification ecosystem. The platform failed to deploy robust safeguards to prevent children under the age of 13 from establishing accounts, while simultaneously lacking oversight mechanisms for teenage users registering without parental knowledge.</p><blockquote><p>&ldquo;The total monetary penalty was calculated based on the severe nature of the infractions, the vast volume of affected Brazilian citizens, and the intrinsic hyper-vulnerability of minors in the digital ecosystem,&rdquo; stated representatives from the ANPD during a press conference following the ruling.</p></blockquote><h2>The Mandatory Compliance Plan: Redefining TikTok in Brazil</h2><p>Beyond the hefty monetary penalty, the ANPD has mandated a legally binding, multi-tiered compliance plan negotiated directly with ByteDance. The measure forces fundamental architecture and algorithmic adjustments to ensure the absolute protection of younger demographics on the platform.</p><h3>1. Mandatory Restrictions for Logged-In Teen Accounts</h3><p>For registered users under the legal age threshold, TikTok must default all privacy parameters to the most restrictive settings possible. Under the new rules, these safety and privacy safeguards cannot be lowered or modified by the minor alone; changes will strictly require approval from verified parents or legal guardians tied through parental supervision controls.</p><h3>2. Strict Overhaul of the 'Guest Mode' Feed</h3><p>For users browsing TikTok without creating or logging into an account, the ANPD has ordered severe limitations to mitigate unvetted exposure:</p><ul><li><strong>Complete Ad and Live Stream Ban:</strong> TikTok must suspend all advertising material, live broadcasts, and direct messaging functionality in guest mode.</li><li><strong>Device Time Capping:</strong> Unauthenticated device usage will be capped at a cumulative total of 12 hours per device, preventing extended unmonitored consumption.</li><li><strong>Restricted Data Telemetry:</strong> Telemetry collection for logged-out sessions will be strictly confined to essential operating metadata, such as device language, regional parameters, and anti-fraud operational metrics.</li></ul><h2>Legal Appeals vs. Immediate Compliance</h2><p>While ByteDance retains the legal right to submit an appeal within a designated window of business days, the regulatory authority clarified that legal appeals against the monetary fine will not halt the mandatory rollout of the compliance framework.</p><p><em>&ldquo;An appeal can certainly be filed against the sanction itself,&rdquo;</em> explained Fabr&iacute;cio Guimar&atilde;es Madruga Lopes, Superintendent of Enforcement at the ANPD. <em>&ldquo;However, in principle, filing an appeal does not delay or impede the execution of the agreed compliance plan.&rdquo;</em></p><h2>A Broader Enforcement Trend Across Latin America</h2><p>This action against ByteDance marks a dramatic escalation in regulatory enforcement across Latin America, positioning Brazil&rsquo;s ANPD alongside major international regulatory bodies such as Europe&rsquo;s EDPB and the U.S. FTC. The ruling comes shortly after another major platform enforcement, where popular VoIP and chat application <strong>Discord suspended all live streaming features in Brazil</strong> following explicit regulatory orders from the ANPD regarding youth protection and content moderation.</p><p>As regulatory scrutiny around algorithmic feeds, data sovereignty, and youth mental health reaches a fever pitch globally, Big Tech operators in South America face an unmistakable message: passive compliance is no longer an option, and non-compliance carries structural as well as financial consequences.</p>
