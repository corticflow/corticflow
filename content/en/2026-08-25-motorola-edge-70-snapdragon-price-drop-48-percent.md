### Display Tech: Shattering Brightness Limits with pOLED

One of the standout hardware highlights of the Motorola Edge 70 is its display panel. Utilizing flexible pOLED display technology, Motorola has pushed maximum outdoor visibility parameters to a staggering 4,500 nits of peak brightness. 

Why does 4,500 nits matter to the user?
1. **Direct Sunlight Legibility:** High outdoor lux environments, such as direct midday sunlight, typically wash out standard AMOLED panels (which range between 1,000 to 2,000 nits). The Edge 70 maintains extreme contrast, legible typography, and vivid color representation regardless of ambient light conditions.
2. **HDR Content Dynamic Range:** When viewing HDR10+ content, the extreme peak brightness allows for hyper-realistic highlights alongside the pitch-black contrast levels inherent to OLED self-emissive subpixels.
3. **Power-Efficient Substrates:** Modern pOLED substrates feature higher lumen-per-watt efficiencies, minimizing battery drain even when driving bright displays during high-performance tasks.
