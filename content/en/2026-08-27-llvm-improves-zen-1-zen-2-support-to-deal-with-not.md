## What Happened

The LLVM/Clang compiler toolchain merged overnight better support for the nearly decade-old AMD Zen 1 processors and also helps with Zen+ and Zen 2 processors too. This latest work thanks to the open-source community is for providing more informed instruction scheduling around PDEP and PEXT that end up being quite costly for these original Zen processors...

### Technical Breakdown & Impact

This update directly impacts the Linux & Open-Source ecosystem, introducing operational changes and new performance standards across the industry.
